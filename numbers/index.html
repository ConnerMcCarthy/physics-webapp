Numbers as rotations

Why does adding up a number's digits tell you whether it divides by 3? Because “mod 3” is a walk around a triangle, and in base 10 every place value — 1, 10, 100, 1000 — lands exactly one corner over. Follow the walk on the left and the algebra on the right; they are the same argument told twice.
